Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 27, 2026

Herbanium is a brewing companion and journal. The short version of this policy: your data stays on your device. We don't have an account system, we don't have a server that holds your information, and the iOS and Android apps don't transmit anything anywhere.

What we collect

Nothing. The Herbanium iOS and Android apps do not collect personal information, do not include analytics, and do not include advertising or tracking SDKs.

Where your data lives

Brew sessions, journal entries, saved recipes, your ingredient cabinet, and the elemental bestiary all live in localStorage on the device you use Herbanium on. They never leave that device unless you choose to delete them (the "start over" button in Profile clears everything).

Third parties

The native iOS and Android apps don't talk to any third-party service. The web version of Herbanium has a feedback form that, if used, sends your written feedback to a service called Formspree so we can read and act on it. That form does not exist in the iOS or Android apps.

Children

Herbanium is a general-audience app and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children — and since we don't collect information from anyone, this remains true by construction.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the "last updated" date at the top of the page will change with it. Any change to what we collect (currently nothing) would also be announced inside the app before it took effect.
