## Long description

(Apple: 4000 chars; Google Play: 4000 chars. Pasted identically
into both. ~250 words below.)

```
Herbanium is a brewing companion and journal — a quiet place to
record your cups, learn what each leaf does, and notice what your
kettle is teaching you.

Compose blends from a curated catalog of true teas, herbs, roots,
and flowers. Pick a mood and let the algorithm suggest what fits;
or build from scratch, with brew temperatures and steep times
calibrated against the literature on each ingredient. Save the
ones worth returning to.

Track every cup. The journal records what you brewed, what you
hoped for, and what actually landed. Over time, patterns emerge —
which ingredients work for you, which combinations don't, what
your evenings prefer.

A small mythic side-game tucks into the kettle: earn elementals
as you brew, write, and explore. Pin five at the top of your
bestiary and turn the rest off in settings if it isn't your thing.

Your data lives on your device. No account, no cloud sync, no
analytics, no ads.

Built for tea drinkers, herb growers, and anyone who wants their
cup to feel intentional. Not medical advice — Herbanium reflects
common literature on each ingredient and should never replace a
clinician. If you're pregnant, nursing, taking prescription
medication, or managing a condition, verify with a qualified
professional before brewing.
```

## App categories

- **Apple:** Primary **Lifestyle**, Secondary **Food & Drink**.
  *Reasoning:* Lifestyle reads as "the kind of app where you keep
  a journal" which is closer to the actual use; Food & Drink as
  secondary catches the tea-search audience.
- **Google Play:** **Lifestyle**.
  *Reasoning:* Play's category list is shorter; Lifestyle is the
  cleanest single bucket. "Health & Fitness" is a fit on register
  but invites medical-advice scrutiny we don't want.

## Age rating questionnaire — sketched answers

The actual questionnaire is per-platform and can change, but a
preview of how each topic should answer:

- Cartoon / fantasy violence: **None.** (Bestiary creatures are
  decorative.)
- Realistic violence: **None.**
- Sexual content / nudity: **None.**
- Profanity / crude humor: **None.**
- Alcohol, tobacco, drug references: **Infrequent / mild.**
  (We catalog herbs with traditional medicinal uses; some
  ingredient histories reference alcohol-based tinctures or
  recreational-adjacent plants. No instructions or encouragement.)
- Mature/suggestive themes: **None.**
- Horror / fear themes: **None.**
- Gambling: **None.**
- User-generated content: **None.** (Journal entries are private,
  on-device only — no upload, no sharing, no other-user
  exposure.)
- Web access: **No** in iOS apps (zero outbound calls).
  **Limited** if the questionnaire counts the WebView platform —
  declare honestly per the actual question text.
- Location: **None.**
- Health & wellness claims: **No medical claims.** The disclaimer
  on every blend page and at the bottom of Profile makes this
  explicit.

Expected ratings: **4+ on iOS**, **Everyone on Google Play**.
