## Addendum — `energy` and `focus` removed (2026-08-02)

An audit found the shipped extraction profile claiming `energy` (2–3)
and `focus` (2) at every brew point, with nothing in this document
supporting either. Checked against the literature rather than adjusted
to match:

> Kennedy DO et al., *Modulation of mood and cognitive performance
> following acute administration of single doses of Melissa officinalis*
> — Neuropsychopharmacology / Pharmacol Biochem Behav.
> https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12062586/

In that randomised, placebo-controlled, double-blind trial, self-rated
**alertness was significantly reduced** at the highest dose, and
calmness significantly increased at 300 mg and 900 mg. Attention
accuracy improved at 600 mg, but that is an accuracy effect under a
sedative, not stimulation. Lemon balm is characterised throughout the
literature as a mild sedative and sleep aid.

So the app was asserting the *opposite* of the evidence. Both effects
removed from the extraction profile. `calm`, `soothing` and `sleepy`
already carry the sourced picture.
